# Family and child accounts (B5)

A parent books for themselves and their dependants (kids, or a partner without their own login) from one account.

- **Data**: a dependant is an ordinary `clients` row with `guardian_client_id` set to the parent. No login, usually no email. Bookings, rosters, check-in, the kiosk, reports and search all work for them unchanged. One level only (a parent can't be a dependant, a dependant can't have dependants), same studio.
- **Client app**: Profile › Family to add, edit or remove dependants (name, optional date of birth, notes or injuries) and sign each one's waiver and health form as the parent. A "Who's coming?" picker shows on classes, workshops and appointments, only when the parent has dependants. Each attendee takes one spot. Classes and workshops can take several people at once (all or nothing, and an error names who needs a pass); a private session is for one person. Removing a dependant archives them, so their history stays, and is blocked while they have upcoming classes.
- **Credits, the one rule**: a dependant spends their own passes first, then the parent's passes that are family-shareable. A pass is shareable if its product says so (copied onto the pass at purchase). If the product doesn't say, packs and drop-ins are shareable; memberships and intro offers are not. Studios can flip it per product in Settings › Products ("Family-shareable"). If nothing is shareable, the dependant needs a pass of their own, and the parent can buy one for them (Family › Buy a pass, or the till).
- **Studio app**: dependants show in rosters under their own name with a "via Sophie" tag, are searchable in Clients (by their name or their parent's), and link to the parent's profile (and the parent's to them). Staff can add dependants and book them in, check in and use the kiosk as usual.
- **Live**: `supabase/migrations/0010_family.sql`. RLS lets a guardian read their dependants, their passes and bookings, edit them and add new ones (no login, waiver never self-declared). RPCs: `save_dependant`, `remove_dependant`, `book_attendees`; `book_class`, `cancel_booking`, `leave_waitlist`, `submit_waiver`, `credit_summary`, `list_client_bookings` and `best_pass` know about guardians. The `book_class` body lives in `1703_integrations_held_spots.sql` (it replaces 0007's), so the same three changes are made there; the appointments RPCs use `app.is_guardian_of` (1101). Isolation tests: `supabase/tests/family_flow.sql`.
- **Gaps**: paying by card online for a dependant's private or workshop isn't built in live (the appointments checkout would book the parent), so live asks the parent to pay at the studio or use a credit. Buying a class pass online for a dependant works (the pass lands on them). No sibling discounts, no per-dependant emails (receipts and reminders go to the parent's email for the parent's bookings only), no age limits.
